# Working agreements

- Plan broad changes before implementation. Keep changes small and independently verifiable.
- Build screens from shared components and reusable patterns; keep application state in the owning feature.
- Public examples use fictional data and local state. Real service calls must be explicit and opt-in.
- Keep private references, research notes, implementation plans, prompts, conversation notes, role claims and completion announcements out of the repository. Preserve licenses and factual attribution.
- Comments explain non-obvious constraints; documentation describes current behavior and limitations. Record substantial architectural decisions in short ADRs.
- Preserve published lab and article URLs. Package APIs may change deliberately when all consumers are updated.
- Keep the main site's business content and integrations in that application; share reusable UI and brand contracts without depending on its checkout.
- Do not push, publish or deploy without authorization. Report what was verified and what remains unchecked.
- AGENTS.md is the only assistant instruction file. Do not create CLAUDE.md or tool-specific copies.
