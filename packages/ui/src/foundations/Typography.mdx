# Typography

Bricolage Grotesque gives consulting headings their shape. Atkinson Hyperlegible
carries reading text and the shared interface. Choose a role by the work the text
does, then keep its hierarchy consistent through the page.

<Canvas of={TypeStories.Reference} />

## Build a reading hierarchy

Use a page title to establish the subject and section headings to make the
content navigable. Heading levels describe the document structure; the type-size
role describes appearance. Do not choose a heading level only to get a larger
font. PageHeader and Section help keep headings consistent in composed pages.

Keep reading text within a comfortable measure using Container's prose width.
Use Stack for spacing between content groups. Check a real paragraph and a long
heading, because a short placeholder does not show wrapping or reading rhythm.

## Keep controls readable

Controls use `--uix-font-ui`, separately from body and display roles. This lets a
brand change its prose while preserving the interface's familiar labels. Write
a label that identifies the value or action, then allow space for longer wording
and translated content.

Use weight and hierarchy deliberately. Reserve emphasis for information someone
needs to find or compare. A whole paragraph in bold makes its important part
harder to distinguish.

## Load the available faces

The consulting adapter includes local font declarations with `font-display:
swap`. Atkinson provides regular, bold and regular italic Latin/Latin Extended
faces. Bricolage provides a variable weight range of 200–800 and an optical-size
axis of 12–96. Characters outside the supplied subsets use the declared fallback
stack. A font-family declaration alone does not load the asset.

Leave the root font size configurable. Inspect narrow layouts, larger text,
font-loading fallback and the languages the application actually serves. The
specimen includes commonly confused characters so you can compare their shapes.
