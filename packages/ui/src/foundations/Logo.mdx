# Logo

Use the supplied asset as a single mark. Choose a variant for its background,
keep its proportions and leave clear space so nearby text or artwork does not
merge with it. The download controls provide the actual files used below.

<Canvas of={LogoStories.Reference} />

## Place the mark

Navy is the default on a light background. Use white on a dark background and
black when the reproduction is restricted to one colour. The red variant is
available for brand artwork; check how it competes with action and status colours
in the surrounding composition.

Keep the mark at least 24 CSS pixels high for digital use. The minimum-size
specimen shows that height with clear space around it. Inspect the result at its
actual rendered size, especially where borders or neighbouring content are close.
These digital examples do not establish print-production requirements.

## Give it an accessible name

A standalone logo needs a useful name. When a logo shares a link with the written
name, use empty image alt text so the name is not announced twice. A decorative
mark can use an empty alt attribute or be hidden from the accessibility tree.

Use the fixed asset rather than rebuilding the mark with text or shapes. Keep
rotation, stretching and decorative shadows out of its presentation.
